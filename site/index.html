Photo tips & privacy

Two minutes here makes a better book — and your photo stays yours.

A photo that paints well

  • Front-facing, eyes open, looking at the camera.
  • One child in the photo, face big in the frame.
  • No sunglasses, and no hat shadow across the face.
  • Plain background if you can — a wall works wonders.
  • An ordinary phone photo is fine. Really.

What we do with it

  • Your photo is used only to paint this one book.
  • We never sell it, post it, or use it to train anything.
  • Our working copy is deleted within 7 days of delivery.
  • The finished book is yours — print it, share it, keep it forever.
  • Please only send a photo of your own child, or one you have the guardian's permission to use.
